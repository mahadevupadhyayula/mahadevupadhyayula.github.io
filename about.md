  <p class="section-eyebrow">Why my background matters</p>
  <h2 id="background-title">I understand both the workflow problem and the product risk.</h2>
  <p>At PayPal, I worked on product initiatives where quality, user trust, and operational clarity mattered. That experience shaped how I think about automation: useful systems need clean inputs, visible logic, measurable handoffs, and safe review points.</p>
  <p>As a founder/operator, I dealt with the same messy work B2B teams face every week: scattered customer context, manual follow-ups, inconsistent records, and workflows that live across documents, spreadsheets, inboxes, and memory.</p>
  <p>That combination is why I do not start with “what can AI do?” I start with the business workflow, the human approval moment, and the output your team needs to trust.</p>

<section class="service-detail-card about-section" aria-labelledby="approach-title">
  <p class="section-eyebrow">My approach</p>
  <h2 id="approach-title">Small, validated, and built around the people who approve the work.</h2>
  <ol class="about-approach-list">
    <li><strong>Map the current workflow.</strong> We identify the messy inputs, repeat decisions, edge cases, and current review steps.</li>
    <li><strong>Define the clean output.</strong> We agree on the fields, format, handoff, and quality bar before building.</li>
    <li><strong>Add extraction and checks.</strong> The system extracts useful structure, flags gaps, and applies validation rules.</li>
    <li><strong>Keep human approval visible.</strong> Your team reviews, edits, and approves before records, drafts, or recommendations move forward.</li>
    <li><strong>Improve from real usage.</strong> We tighten prompts, rules, and workflow steps based on actual examples from your team.</li>
  </ol>
</section>
