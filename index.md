<section class="measure-section" aria-labelledby="measure-title">
  <p class="section-eyebrow">I tie every workflow to a business measure</p>
  <h2 id="measure-title">If the workflow cannot show a credible path to value, it is not the right place to automate.</h2>
  <p class="section-support">I start with the current work: how often it happens, where time is lost, what gets returned or reworked, and what a mistake costs. Then we define the smallest measure a pilot needs to improve—without increasing reviewer burden or creating uncontrolled risk.</p>
  <div class="measure-steps" aria-label="Pilot measurement approach">
    <article><span class="step-number">01</span><h3>Establish the baseline</h3><p>Measure the current time, rework, exceptions, and volume.</p></article>
    <article><span class="step-number">02</span><h3>Test one workflow</h3><p>Build a focused workflow with clear review points.</p></article>
    <article><span class="step-number">03</span><h3>Decide with evidence</h3><p>Continue, refine, or stop based on the agreed operational measure.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="measure-row" aria-label="What we might measure">
    <span><strong>Implementation:</strong> fewer missing requirements at kickoff; less delivery rework</span>
    <span><strong>Quality:</strong> faster triage-ready escalation packages; fewer engineering returns</span>
    <span><strong>Product:</strong> faster evidence synthesis; fewer unsupported product decisions</span>
    <span><strong>Revenue/CRM:</strong> fewer returned CRM changes; less manual research and record cleanup</span>
  </div>
</section>
