<section class="workflow-cta" aria-labelledby="project-flow-title">
  <p class="section-eyebrow">Prototype, then decide</p>
  <h2 id="project-flow-title">The sprint produces evidence for the next decision—not a promise of a production system.</h2>
  <p>The goal is to test how one workflow should handle inputs, validation, exceptions, human approval, and structured output. The result should make it easier to decide whether to stop, iterate, or plan a separate implementation phase.</p>
