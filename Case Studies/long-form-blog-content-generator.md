**Portfolio prototype: Turning source documents into SEO/AEO blog drafts, visuals, and publishing assets**

Service relevance: content automation, source-grounded drafting, human-reviewed marketing workflows.

---

## Problem

Marketing teams often have strong source material but limited time to turn it into high-quality long-form content.

Customer notes, internal documents, webinar transcripts, product pages, and research links sit unused because converting them into a polished blog requires strategy, writing, editing, SEO review, and asset preparation.

## Buyer / user

**Primary buyer:** B2B founders, content leads, demand generation teams, and agencies that need consistent blog output without lowering quality.

**Primary users:** Content marketers, subject-matter experts, founders, and editors.

## Workflow pain

Long-form content production is slow because every article requires multiple handoffs.

Common pain points include:

- Source documents are scattered across tools.
- Writers need time to extract the useful points.
- SEO briefs are created separately from drafting.
- AI drafts often sound generic without source grounding.
- Editors still need to check structure, claims, and brand fit.
- Visual and distribution assets are created after the article instead of as part of the workflow.

## Inputs

The workflow can use content inputs such as:

- Source documents or notes
- Product pages and positioning docs
- SME interview transcripts
- Webinar or podcast transcripts
- Target keyword or topic
- Audience and funnel stage
- Brand voice rules
- Internal linking priorities
- Example articles or preferred structure

## AI workflow

**Messy Inputs → AI Extraction → Validation → Human Approval → Clean Output**

The agent turns source material into a structured content package.

The workflow can produce:

- Source summary
- Search-intent analysis
- SEO/AEO-oriented outline
- Question-based headings
- Draft article sections
- Suggested examples and proof points
- Internal-link recommendations
- Meta title and description
- Social post variations
- Visual brief or image prompt ideas

The system emphasizes source-grounded content rather than generic topic expansion.

## Human approval / validation

Human review is built into the workflow before publication.

The editor or SME validates:

- Accuracy of claims
- Whether the draft reflects the source material
- Brand voice and positioning
- SEO/AEO structure
- Missing examples or proof
- Sections that need rewriting
- Visual directions before production

The agent prepares the content system; the human approves the final editorial judgment.

## Outputs

The approved output is a publish-ready content package:

- Long-form blog draft
- SEO/AEO outline and metadata
- Source-to-section mapping
- Editor checklist
- Internal-link suggestions
- Social distribution snippets
- Visual brief or prompt set

## Business value

The system helps marketing teams convert existing knowledge into useful content faster.

It can:

- Reduce first-draft production time
- Improve consistency across articles
- Keep content grounded in source documents
- Support SEO and AI-answer readability
- Create reusable briefs and distribution assets from one workflow

## What this proves

This project shows how AI can support content operations when it is constrained by source material and human editorial approval.

The repeatable pattern is not one-click publishing. It is a structured workflow that turns messy source inputs into a reviewable content package.
